# Airy item correlation — broad presentation continuation

## Delivered

The shared character now uses one silhouette, two broad material planes and one material-specific gesture instead of stacked shell plates, bevel cages, a second eye mask, ornamental shoulder rigs and decorative spark routing. Ten shell materials retain distinct finishes and physical profiles. The seven real core mechanisms retain their protected interiors and actual-state readouts, but lose nested carrier rings, screw plates and mounting cages. Head hardware retains the previous eight recognizable silhouettes without duplicate mounting ornament.

The same production renderer serves live play, portraits, practice and the shop. No separate prettified replacement character was introduced for evidence. Family weapon artwork remains authoritative; removing the redundant family shoulder rig does not remove family weapons or change their rules.

The shop uses a continuous warm-paper stage, soft contact shadow, larger two-column portraits, quieter stock labels and a flatter comparison surface. Luminous slash/dash portraits retain a flat dark contrast field: airy must not mean washed out. The wordless Look → Action → Result rail remains. Isolated head/core/eye/tail studies show the part beside its fitted character. Secondary tools stay behind the existing disclosure. Names, benefits and prices remain visible; this is not an attempt to make players memorize unlabeled icons.
